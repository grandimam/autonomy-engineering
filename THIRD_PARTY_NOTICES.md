## OpenAI Performance Factory visuals

The files in [the case study's diagrams directory](case-studies/openai-performance-factory/diagrams/README.md) are OpenAI figures or browser captures of OpenAI's published visuals. They come from [How OpenAI’s Performance Factory keeps ChatGPT fast](https://openai.com/index/how-openai-performance-factory-keeps-chatgpt-fast/), by Okay Zed and Cyrus Forbes, published November 19, 2026.

Copyright remains with the respective rights holders. No separate open-source license for these visuals was identified during capture. Attribution and local storage do not relicense the material under MIT.

The [capture manifest](case-studies/openai-performance-factory/source/manifest.json) records the origin and integrity of each asset. The full article text and full embedded video are not included.
